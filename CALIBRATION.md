# Calibration report: sim vs real NBA data

The engine is calibrated against real Basketball-Reference data (through the 2025-26 season), not memory.

- **Data:** `sumitrodatta/bball-reference-datasets` (public GitHub repo, a Basketball-Reference scrape). Clone it next to this project.
- **Targets:** `python3 scripts/real-targets.py [path/to/Data]` computes everything below and writes `data/real-targets.json`.
- **Comparison:** `node scripts/real-compare.js 80 3` simulates 3 leagues x 80 seasons and prints real vs sim. `node scripts/ovr-equalize.js` checks that equal overall ratings give equal production across archetypes.
- **"perf"** is one production number used for both: `pts*(1+2.2*(TS%-0.57)) + 1.15*reb + 1.5*ast + 2*(stl+blk) - 1.1*tov`.

## Known gaps (honest list)

- **Sustained stars:** 2.1 players per draft class with 5+ All-Star seasons (real 1.27); any All-Star per class 4.5 (real 5.35). Three-time All-Stars 2.9 (real 2.73).
- **Mid-tier production is 4-10% high** (ranks 10-150 perf; the 24th-best player is 40.4 vs 37.0), mostly assists, blocks and rebounds. Scoring by rank is within 3-10%. The top 3 players match.
- **30+ ppg players** (1.7 vs 2.1) and the top scorer (31.5 vs 32.6) run slightly low; ppg at ranks 35-150 is 5-12% high.
- **Year-to-year change** in team wins is 9.4 vs 8.5 and win persistence is 0.52 vs 0.60.
- **Archetype templates** still miss real defenders by about -1 point and -1 assist per 36, and real scorers/shooters by about +1 point per 36.
- **Triple-double rates** cannot be checked from season files; they rest on known career figures.
- **Salaries** are not calibrated (no salary file).
- **Real-roster ratings** blend 65% stats with 35% my prior, and some 2026 depth charts were conflicting preseason reports.

## Game-to-game spread (real play-by-play game logs, 2001-2024)
`scripts/real-gamelogs.py` rebuilds box scores from `shufinskiy/nba_data`; `scripts/gamelog-compare.js` feeds each real player-season average into `genGame`.
Points noise was too tight (sd/mean 0.234 vs real 0.333, 40+/50+ games 3-8x too rare for 18-28 ppg players). Noise raised to 0.29: sd/mean ≈ 0.31, 30+/40+/50+ rates within ~10-20% of real across ppg bins. Rebounds/assists spread already matched. Triple-double rate at the very top lines is still ~15% light.

**TD correlation:** rebounds and assists now share a per-game "engagement" factor (sd 0.13) on top of the minutes factor, so big boards and big dimes cluster like real triple-double nights. Overall TD rate for 59 real TD-capable seasons: 16.3% sim vs 17.8% real (was 15.1%); rebound/assist spread still ≈ real (0.461/0.523 vs 0.452/0.522).

1,318 college draft prospects 2009-2024 (RealGM/BBRef/Barttorvik/247), with RSCI recruit rank (400 = unranked, 208 of them), class year at declaration and per-40 college stats. **No pick numbers, no star ratings, no non-draft recruits**, so star-rating odds (the 1★ most common split) still cannot be calibrated.

## Using the prospect data (applied)
- **Age at draft** now follows recruit tier: each class's college prospects are ranked by ceiling and mapped to the real RSCI tiers (top-5 → 90% freshmen … unranked → 75% seniors); 26% of each class are international/pro-pathway prospects with a young skew. Class quality is unchanged within noise (All-Stars per class 4.6 vs real 5.3; 3+ 3.4 vs 2.7).
- **College lines** (`scripts/college-check.js`): real freshmen drafted from the top-5 recruits average 14.3 pts / 6.3 reb / 2.2 ast in 28 mpg (all freshmen 13.1 / 5.6 / 2.1). The sim's 5★ freshman was 10.0 / 7.2 / 1.8 in 27.7; after raising star-scaled minutes/usage and lowering the college rebound multiplier it is 12.9 / 6.9 / 2.1 in 29.6. 1-3★ lines are left low on purpose (most of them never get drafted, so the real file has no comparison).
- Still unavailable: real star-rating odds (needs the cbbd API key via hoopR; no R in this environment).

## 30k / 10k / 10k club
`scripts/big3-check.js`. Real: only LeBron has 30,000 pts + 10,000 reb + 10,000 ast. Before: 10% of generational careers and 7% of an all-5★ elite sample got there (generational assists were 8.4k per career vs ~5.5k for real all-time greats). Now generational archetypes pass/rebound a little less (ast ×0.88, reb ×0.97), a hidden "big three" career is rare (4% generational, 1.5% dominator, 0.4% others), and everyone else sees the last category fade late in the career plus a backstop that stops the final total at ~9,860. Result in the elite sample: 0 of 180 careers (≈4% of generational careers can still do it); 30k points alone is still 38% of that elite sample.

## Match ratings (Sofascore-style)
Sofascore's own page could not be fetched here (DNS-blocked); a search summary confirms: 3.0-10 scale, everyone starts at 6.5, ratings move with each action, minimum ~10 minutes. The coefficients are **fitted to the eight reference box scores per tier you supplied** (nightmare 4.4 → 50-piece 9.5):
`rating = 6.5 + (0.12·points-over-expected + 0.034·reb + 0.076·ast + 0.081·stl + 0.086·blk − 0.081·tov − 0.2·min/36) × min(1, min/14)`, where points-over-expected = pts − 1.1·(FGA + 0.44·FTA). The eight examples come out at 5.3 / 5.8 / 6.8 / 6.3 / 7.6 / 8.6 / 9.5 / 9.0 (targets 4.4 / 5.5 / 6.6 / 6.5 / 7.5 / 8.6 / 9.4 / 9.5; fouls and +/- are not modelled, which is most of the miss on the first two).
Per-game shot volume is now loosely tied to points (bricky nights exist), so bad games are possible. Not modelled: pace/game-context adjustment and +/-. Sim distribution (`scripts/rating-check.js`): role players avg 6.7, 12-20 ppg 7.0, 20-26 ppg 7.2, 26+ ppg 7.4 (p5 5.9, p95 9.0; 9+ in ~5% of games, <5 in ~0.4%).

## Rookie scoring
`scripts/rookie-check.js`: generational rookies could average 26-29 ppg. Real rookies since 2000 top out at ~21-23.5 (Iverson 23.5, Griffin 22.5, Wembanyama 21.4, Luka 21.2, LeBron 20.9). ## Round of fixes after play-testing (generational careers)
- **Assist cliff (6.9 → 0.6 at 35-36):** the "last category of 30k/10k/10k" backstop clamped assists hard. It is now a 2-3 season glide (down to 45%) and, only as a last resort, a season-ending injury that ends the year early. 10k-assist careers 11 → 3 of 180, none reach all three. Post triple-double years also decay ~20%/yr instead of dropping from 10.4 to 5.7 overnight.
- **Scoring costs playmaking:** above 27 ppg, assists fall 7% and rebounds 4% per extra point (floors 52%/66%), for NPCs and the user's 32-36 ppg years. No more 35/9/8 seasons.
- **Awards:** MVP vote noise 6 → 3, repeat-winner fatigue 4 → 2.5, +3/+7/+10 triple-double narrative bonus; All-NBA noise 4.5 → 3. 30/10/10 seasons win MVP ~68% (was ~35%). Finals MVP now goes to the champion's best player ~90% of the time (blend of Finals and regular-season lines, 10% bonus if best on the team). Superstar teams are no longer capped by the 54+ win suppression and get a small playoff power bump. Generational careers (60-120 sims): MVP 1.9, FMVP 1.7, titles 2.25 (real GOAT-tier avg ~2.7 / 2.8 / 4; auto-play makes dumb team choices, so human play should land higher).
- **Playoff scoring:** stars now get a +0 to +5.5% playoff scoring lift above ovr 84 (Jordan +3, LeBron +1.4, Durant +2.2, Curry +1.4 ppg).
- **Sofascore ratings:** refit with season-average anchors (30/10/10 ≈ 8.3-8.5, Jokic-style ≈ 8.7); 30/10/10 seasons now average 8.5. Role players ~6.9, 20-26 ppg ~7.6, 26+ ppg ~7.9 (`scripts/rating-check.js`, `scripts/mvp-check.js`).
- **Game plan:** Balanced / Scoring priority / Pass & rebound / Defend more (chosen on the pre-season screen). Pass & rebound roughly doubles triple-double seasons, Defend more roughly triples DPOYs (`/tmp` check: 40 careers each).
- Season tables and career totals now show average match rating (regular season and playoffs).

## Mid-season game plan + generational resume
- **Game plan during the season:** the four plans (Balanced / Scoring / Pass & rebound / Defend) now switch at any game, including the playoffs. The season is still simulated up front, but on a switch the engine restores the tip-off snapshot and replays it with the new plan: league results are identical (the user's games use their own random stream), games already shown stay exactly as they were, the remaining games use the new plan, and the season record is recomputed from the tiles (`scripts/style-switch-test.js`).
- **Generational resume (`scripts/mvp-check.js`, 200 careers):** MVP 2.67, Finals MVP 2.83, titles 4.03 (targets 2.7 / 2.8 / 4.0). Levers: superstar teams pull talent (depth bonus up to +6 at 0.7 per ovr point above 84), +10% playoff power bump, MVP vote noise 2.4, win-total bonus, triple-double narrative bonus, Finals MVP leans on the team's best player.

## 50/40/90 seasons
`scripts/5040-check.js`: elite shooter careers averaged 3.45 seasons of 50% FG / 40% 3P / 90% FT (real: Nash x4 on ~4 threes a game, Bird, Dirk, Durant, Curry, Kyrie once each, about a dozen ever). When a season's shooting line lands on all three marks, one of them now slips just under (2-15 points of a percent) with 87% (6+ threes a game), 75% (3.5-6) or 65% (under 3.5) probability. Now 0.35 per elite shooter career, 0.00 for everyone else, with plenty of 49/39/89 near-misses.

## Scoring title / MVP eligibility fixes
- A mid-season game-plan switch re-ran the season, so the "Scoring Champion (30.6)" text and title came from the re-run line while the tiles said 31.2. After a switch the scoring title, its text, the league ranks and the leaders table are now recomputed from the final tile averages against the league's best other scorer (`scripts/style-switch-test.js`).
- Removed the invented 36-team-win minimum for MVP. Only the real 65-game rule remains; team wins still count through the vote formula.

## Round 5: stability, awards, scoring titles, usage, phone layout
- **Career highs / best game** per player on the career page (regular season + playoffs; stored per season so they survive the match log being replaced).
- **Stable primes (`scripts/stability-check.js`):** form now carries over year to year and is calmer for stars (AR(0.5), sd 3.5%), teammate impact and team pace settle gradually, stars' rating noise after 23 is cut 40%, and a visible 4-year peak (peak age 25-29, +9% at the top, -5% at the tails) shapes elite careers. Prime year-to-year change 2.7 → 2.0 ppg (real LeBron ≈ 1.8), changes of 4+ ppg 24% → 12% (most of them are the planned 32+/triple-double seasons), up/down zig-zags of 3+ ppg 0.95 → 0.30 per career.
- **Playoff scoring:** star playoff/regular-season ppg ratio sd 0.137 → 0.112, floor 0.82 for stars (no more 17-19 ppg playoff runs in a prime), small +0-5.5% star lift. Playoff ppg ≥30 in 25-30% of elite seasons.
- **MVP plausibility:** candidates must be top-5 in production, have 44+ team wins and be a real scorer/playmaker (22+ ppg, 9.5+ apg, or 11+ reb with 18+ pts); weaker candidates only fill out the ballot. Sim MVP winners: 25.2 ppg at the 5th percentile, none under 22, team wins ≥ 44. Best producer in the league wins 93% of the time.
- **Finals MVP:** the champion's leading Finals scorer (within 86% of the top scorer), or an elite defender (3+ stocks/g) with a strong line; teammates' Finals lines are drawn around their season averages.
- **Scoring titles:** the soft cap that made 30.2-31 ppg rare is now a smooth curve (no 30-31 gap), and if the league's best scorer would end under ~31, the best other scorer is lifted to the target: top scorer ppg median 33, 4% under 31 (real: 2 of the last 12 years).
- **Even-handed season:** each season's games are spread by quality (low-discrepancy order) so a hot first half no longer fades; half-season average is within ~0.3 ppg of the final.
- **Usage and minutes by team quality:** stars on bad teams get up to +5.5% usage and 38-40 mpg, stars on good teams 33-34 mpg. Elite usage cap 35.4% → 37%.
- **Team page** with teammates' lines, OVR and match ratings, plus **phone layout v2**: sim controls in the flow (no floating bar), quick stats under them, one panel at a time (Box / Shots / Stats / Defense / Plan), standings fit the screen.
- Generational careers (200 sims): MVP 2.4, Finals MVP 2.6, titles 3.9 (targets 2.7 / 2.8 / 4.0; the MVP rules are now strict, so MVP sits ~10% under).

## v26: realism test (sim careers vs real careers of the same archetype)

`scripts/realism-run.sh [N]` (wraps `scripts/realism-test.js`) creates N careers per archetype, keeps those that became notable players of that archetype
(the real cohort rules of `scripts/real-blueprints.py`), and compares them with the real careers in `data/real-careers.json` on ~19 features:
peak-5 ppg/rpg/apg, TS%, minutes, best season, 30-ppg seasons, age of best season, debut age, year-to-year swing, games played, All-Star / All-NBA / MVP / All-Defense,
career length, final age, career points.
- Feature score = (1 - KS distance) / (1 - KS distance a perfect sim shows at the same sample size); archetype realism = mean feature score.
- Plausible careers = share of sim careers whose key stats all sit inside the range real careers of that archetype have covered.
- Real stats are era-adjusted to the 2022-25 league (per-game stats scaled by league averages, TS% shifted), so 1990s and 2020s players compare fairly.
- Career totals use finished careers: retired by 2024, or debuted by 2008. Usage is shown but not scored (era adjustment does not translate it cleanly).

Fixes from this round: real-data debut computed before the 1980 cut (Kareem etc. no longer counted with half careers); the autopilot stays in college
like real recruits of its star rating (2009-24 draft ages); more and longer major injuries; All-Star and All-Defense selection now use logistic models fitted to
2001-25 voting (`scripts/real-allstar-model.py`; 12 All-Stars per conference, reputation from last year's selection, steals weigh most for All-Defense);
retirement depends less on talent; archetype TS% offsets fitted to the real (era-adjusted) archetypes; scorers' perimeter defence up (real-like steals);
role players' minutes swing more year to year.
- Copy check (v26+): a sim career counts as a copy when it sits closer to one finished real career (standardized over 15 career stats) than the 5th percentile of real careers to their own nearest real twin, so ~5% is the real baseline. First run: 12% overall (gens 7%, scorers 6%, defenders 3%, two-way 10%, shooters 19%, dominators 23%); the shooter and dominator "copies" are of role-player profiles (Wesley Matthews, Grant Hill, Fat Lever) and come from sim careers clustering, with only 11-14 finished real careers setting the line.

## v36: a real shot chart

The dashboard's shot chart used to scatter made and missed marks at random in proportion to the season totals (re-drawn whenever the attempt
count changed), so it never showed where shots were actually taken. Now every game in the season log carries its own shots (`sh`, built in
`buildMatchLog` from that game's box score by `shotsFor`): exactly FGA shots, 3PA of them threes, FGM made, 3PM of the makes threes. Shots are
packed one integer each (x | y << 9 | made << 18 | zone << 19) and drawn on their own random stream (seeded by season and schedule slot), so
nothing else in the season moves and a mid-season game-plan replay draws the same spots for the same box.
- Where the twos come from follows the player around the 2023-24 league mix of two-point tries (restricted area ~50%, rest of the paint ~29%,
  mid-range ~21%): inside vs mid-range rating and position shift it (a rim-running center ~70 / 24 / 6). Corner threes are 17-27% of threes by position.
  Over 3,394 sim games: rim 53%, paint 28%, mid 20% of twos, 22% of threes from the corners.
- Which tries go in is drawn by each zone's real accuracy, sharpened because a game's makes are a fixed number shared out without replacement:
  rim / paint / mid 65.7 / 42.5 / 42.6% (real 66 / 43 / 41), corner / above the break 41.3 / 37.0% (real 39 / 35.5; these careers shoot threes a
  little better than the league, the corner edge is the real x1.1).
- The chart shows every shot of the games played so far, regular season and playoffs as separate charts (it switches to the playoffs when they
  start), with the last game's shots in gold. Saves from before v36 have no stored shots: the chart draws them from each game's box the same way.
- Box-score fixes: 3% of games had more two-point makes than two-point tries (a chart cannot be drawn from that); such games take the
  shortfall off the three-point tries. And a season's box scores, drawn game by game, drifted from its shooting line (FG% ~1 point low for
  regulars, 10-20 points for low-volume players: a 1.7-ppg season showed 33% in the boxes and 53% in the record, and a different split of
  points between threes, twos and free throws), so the career page could show a different FG% from the games. `fitShooting` now puts each
  season's boxes on its line with every game keeping its points: first makes (a two plus a free throw become a three or back, a two becomes two
  free throws or back) until the season's made threes and free throws are the line's, then misses (attempts added to or taken off games) until
  its 3P%, FG% and FT% are the line's to the nearest attempt; game ratings are recomputed (playoffs: the playoff line). The season record's
  shooting is then the sum of its boxes (`shootingFromLog`, also used after a mid-season game-plan replay). Over 482 seasons: record = box
  sums exactly, every game's shots = its box; over 27,337 games every box adds up (points = 2 FGM + 3PM + FTM, makes <= attempts). The makes
  step needs the line's made threes and free throws (the season line now carries `tpm` / `ftm`): a big's line shows no 3P% under 0.3 threes a
  game but still has a few makes.
- Realism unchanged at 89%, and the season numbers it reads are the line's again: peak TS / share of shots from three / FT% per archetype within
  0.01 of v35 (gens 64.36 / .443 / .792, shooters 59.28 / .576 / .894, defenders 57.57 / .026 / .605).
- The UI test checks the chart against the box scores at seven points of a season (3 games, after a game-plan replay, the All-Star break, the
  end of the regular season, playoff game 1, back on the regular-season chart, 30 games into season 2): chart totals = summed box scores =
  marks drawn, desktop and phone.
- Phone layout: once the playoffs added a sixth tab, the tab row's width stretched the whole dashboard past the screen and the page clipped
  the right edge (stat chips, buttons, the court). The dashboard column is now `minmax(0, 1fr)`, so the tab row scrolls inside it.
